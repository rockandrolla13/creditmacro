HY HPC shows the **success-to-the-successful crowding signature** (supply + inflows → weight
growth + outperformance + tightening). High attention × reinforcing-loop dominance ⇒ likely
*already priced* — a **watchlist**, not a promote, until the reversal condition is named.

## Core causal hypothesis
Reinforcing loop: performance → inflows → demand → tightening → more issuance → bigger index
weight → forced index/ETF buying → performance. The balancing limit (supply indigestion /
redemptions) is the reversal the theme must watch.

## Causal graph
`HPC supply (FLOW)` → `index weight (STOCK)` ; `performance → inflows → tightening → performance`
(reinforcing, reflexive). Balancing: `wider spread → higher funding cost → less issuance`.

## Operational axes
`HY HPC OAS − HY index OAS` (HPC at 295bp; index weight 2.68%). A dispersion/relative axis, not
an outright level.

## Confounders
Standing crowding / risk premium (the +9.99% may be flow, not value); survivorship of marquee
issuers; single-name concentration.

## Falsifiers
HPC index weight reverses on net redemptions (crowding unwinds); spreads *widen* as supply keeps
growing (supply indigestion dominates).
