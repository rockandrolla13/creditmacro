# AI capex funding credit ecosystem

## Current belief
The AI-capex cycle has created **one inter-related credit complex** best read as *related
obligations* across risk profiles, not a single directional bet. The first-order opportunity
is **relative value across the legs**, gated by the standing crowding/risk-premium confounder.

## Source facts vs agent synthesis
**Source facts (JPM 2026-05-11):** 27 issuers / >$450bn ([[evidence:jpm-2026-05-11-001]]);
"related obligations / different risk profiles" framing ([[evidence:jpm-2026-05-11-002]]);
ecosystem links hyperscalers/DCs/HY HPC/neoclouds/private AI ([[evidence:jpm-2026-05-11-005]]);
hyperscaler-to-IG-project 105bp, hyperscaler-to-HY-project 183bp ([[evidence:jpm-2026-05-11-003]]/[[evidence:jpm-2026-05-11-004]]);
funding extending into loans (CRWV) ([[evidence:jpm-2026-05-11-015]]).
**Agent synthesis (not in source):** that the complex loads on *one* AI-funding factor, so the
legs are not independent bets — a diversification illusion to watch in any multi-leg RV.

## Main developments
A sizeable, index-relevant AI-credit complex now exists and is still being funded; the channel
is broadening from IG bonds to HY and leveraged loans.

## Key events

## Core causal hypothesis
AI capex (flow) → debt-funded data-center buildout → new issuance fans out across hyperscaler
IG, data-center project, and HY HPC → measurable spread *relationships* between the legs.

## Causal graph

`spread relationships (105bp IG, 183bp HY)`. Reflexive watch: narrative → index inclusion →
flows → tightening (see [[data-center-index-inclusion-technicals]], [[hy-hpc-crowding-and-supply]]).

(≈183bp). Differentials only — no curve points, no legs.

## Confounders

## Falsifiers

"relationship" is not a real, tradeable RV.
