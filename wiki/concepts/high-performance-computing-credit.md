# High-performance-computing (HPC) credit

## What it is
HY credit of compute-infrastructure issuers (neoclouds / GPU-lessors / AI-infra) funding the
AI buildout — bonds and increasingly leveraged loans (see [[limited-syndication]]).

## Mechanics (how to reason)
A fast-growing, supply-heavy HY cohort whose returns can be dominated by **flows and index-weight
growth** rather than fundamentals — the success-to-the-successful crowding signature (performance
→ inflows → tightening → performance). Read level *and* the reversal condition (supply
indigestion / redemptions), not level alone.

## Operational-axis form
`HY HPC OAS − HY index OAS` (a dispersion axis); index-weight as the accumulating stock.

## Confounders
Standing crowding / risk premium, survivorship of marquee issuers, single-name concentration,
GPU residual-value risk.
