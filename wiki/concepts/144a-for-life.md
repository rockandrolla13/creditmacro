# 144A-for-life

## What it is
A bond issued under Rule 144A **without registration rights** — it stays 144A for its life
rather than converting to a registered (SEC) bond.

## Mechanics (how to reason)
144A-for-life affects **index eligibility and the investor base**: some benchmarks/mandates
restrict or differ on 144A-for-life paper, which interacts directly with the
[[index-inclusion-technical]] (whether a name attracts index-tracking demand) and with
liquidity. A structural input to "which index does this sit in", not a value signal by itself.

## Operational-axis form
Not an axis — an **eligibility/structure flag** that conditions the index-inclusion technical and
liquidity scoring for a name.

## Confounders
Conflating eligibility-driven spread with credit-driven spread.
