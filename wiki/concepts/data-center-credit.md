# Data-center credit

## What it is
The credit of entities that build/own data centers — issued as corporate IG (hyperscaler-
adjacent), **project/SPV bonds** (often non-recourse, single-/multi-tenant), and ABS. A new
index sub-sector as of 2026 (see [[data-center-index-inclusion-technicals]]).

## Mechanics (how to reason)
Cashflows rest on tenant contracts (PPAs/leases), utilisation, and power availability; recovery
rests on the durability of the physical asset (the building + the GPU/equipment). Spread vs the
broader Technology sector reflects structure (recourse/subordination), duration, and a liquidity
premium — not only default risk.

## Operational-axis form
`data-center sub-sector OAS − Technology OAS` (duration-adjusted); `hyperscaler IG OAS − DC
project OAS` (see [[hyperscaler-project-bond-basis-mechanics]]). Differentials, never levels.

## Confounders
Duration, recourse/subordination, single-tenant concentration, GPU/equipment residual-value
risk, the standing credit risk premium.
