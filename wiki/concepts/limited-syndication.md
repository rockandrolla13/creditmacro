# Limited syndication

## What it is
A primary placement to a narrow set of investors (club / limited group) rather than a broad
syndicate — common for novel AI-infra and HPC/loan financings.

## Mechanics (how to reason)
Narrow placement → concentrated holders → **thinner secondary liquidity** and weaker price
discovery. The quoted spread may not be a tradeable level for size; liquidity is a first-class
gate (Xantium-style scoring) before any RV expression. The AI funding channel extending into
leveraged loans (e.g. CRWV) is a limited-syndication signal.

## Operational-axis form
Not an axis itself — a **liquidity caveat** on any axis built from these instruments (cap the
strategy-family confidence via data sufficiency / liquidity).

## Confounders
Apparent tightness that is really illiquidity; marks vs tradeable levels.
