## **Global In the Flow October Recap** 

## **Performance – All Tricks, No Treats** 

October was a scary month. Global equity markets saw a ~2 sigma move, leaving the global index down 3.5% YTD and down 10.1% from the highs in January. S&P 500 total returns fell by 6.8% while EM equities fell by 8.7% on the month. Rates, FX and credit moves were orderly in comparison, with UST 10Y total returns at -0.4% and US HY excess returns at -1.6%. The same dynamic occurred in vol, with a sharp repricing in equity vol contrasting with rates, credit and FX vol, which remain near the lower end of their 10-year range. Within global equities, value outperformed growth and utilities was the bestperforming global sector in October. Trade-weighted USD rallied 2.4% on the month, with USD appreciating against most currencies, with the notable exceptions of JPY and BRL.
