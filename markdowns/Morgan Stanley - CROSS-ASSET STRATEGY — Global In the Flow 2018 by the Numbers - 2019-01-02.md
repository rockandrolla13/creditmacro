## **Performance – 2018, a Tough Year, Full of Surprises** 

Nearly all the major assets posted negative returns in 2018, some even worse than 2008. 2018 was also a year where large surprises happened more often: the largest 1-day spike in VIX history, largest 1- day spike in Italy 2-year yields since 1993, and multiple political risk events globally. Transitioning from a year of good returns and low volatility in 2017 where most asset classes made money, 2018 was undoubtedly a tough year, with virtually no major assets beating inflation. We have had the worst global equity annual returns and largest annual rise in VIX since 2008. Most notably, 2018 saw the worst December returns for S&P 500 since 1930s and worst December excess returns for US credit since 1989.

## **Sentiment – Turning Fearful** 

Sentiment across several risk indicators has moved closer to ‘fear’ territory. The MS Global Risk Demand Index* fell to -2.98 and VIX rose to 36% in December before ending the year at -1.53 and 25.4%, respectively. Retail sentiment, a 4-week moving average of the AAII bull-bear indicator, turned negative for the first time in 2018.
