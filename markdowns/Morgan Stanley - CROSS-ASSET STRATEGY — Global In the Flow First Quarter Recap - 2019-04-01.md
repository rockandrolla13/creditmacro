## **Performance – S&P 500’s Best 1Q Since 1998** 

## **Market Is Pricing in Fed Cuts over the Next 12M** 

The first quarter saw strong returns across almost every asset class. The S&P 500 posted its strongest 1Q since 1998, gaining 13.6% since the start of this year. 10-year US Treasuries gained 2.9%, Bund yields fell below zero and credit spreads saw broad tightening. The last quarter to see US equities gain more than 10% and Treasuries to gain more than 2.5% was 3Q10. We’d note, however, that these returns were highly front-loaded, and risk asset returns slowed down materially in March. By country, China equities have had the strongest performance YTD (up 27.0% in USD). By sector, tech has remained the strongest-performing sector in ACWI last quarter, while real estate was the best-performing sector in March. The trade-weighted USD fell 0.4% in 1Q19. Brent remains one of the strongest commodities YTD (+28.8%).
