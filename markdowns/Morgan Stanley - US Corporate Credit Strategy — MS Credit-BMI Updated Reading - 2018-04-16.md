## **Drivers of credit-BMI:** 

**1. Sentiment:** Sentiment has trended a bit weaker on the month. That said, MS equity strategists are tactically bullish based on expectations of a solid reporting season, among other factors (detailed here). Hence if that view plays out, we'd expect a more positive reading from this signal. 

**2. Volatility** : Volatility is slightly elevated relative to a month ago, driving a cautious position at a score of -1.5. However, it's worth noting that current implied volatility is lower than in early February and has generally trended lower the past three weeks. Note the volatility signal has a better track record for long (vs. short) directional recommendations. 

**3. Liquidity** : Our liquidity signal is in neutral territory, driven by a relatively constant LIBOR OIS (5-yr) for the month. Our interest rate strategists expect LIBOR OIS to compress going forward (detailed here), which would support a more positive reading from the signal. 

**4. Momentum** : Momentum is in neutral territory, a signal based on a moving average of 4M HY excess returns adjusted for volatility. IG has underperformed HY recently, and we find that using IG excess return momentum for this signal would have resulted in a more cautious reading (vs. neutral).

## Methodology 

## **Methodology** 

**Credit Bond Market Indicator** is a framework to help make tactical market calls on US credit based on four key factors - volatility, momentum, sentiment, and liquidity. We identify raw variables for each of the four key factors, and then convert each raw variable into a z-score. Next, we use a cumulative distribution function to assign scores of -10 to 10 over time to that z-score. Scores range from - 10 (max short) to +10 (max long). We eliminate “weak” signals that hover near zero in the range of -1 to 1. To combine the factors into one credit-BMI, we take the simple average of the four signals. We find more extreme scores have higher success rates in predicting the direction of credit excess returns and are associated with larger magnitude spread changes. Hence, the absolute level of the signal guides our degree of conviction for a tactical recommendation.
