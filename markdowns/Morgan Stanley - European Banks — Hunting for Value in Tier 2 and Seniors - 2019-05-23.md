## **Recommendations and risks** 

Non-preferred senior spreads are yet to find a stable landing point between Tier 2 and preferred senior, with differences varying by bank and geography. Scrubbing Tier 2 and seniors, we see the most value in French NPS, Spanish Tier 2 and preferred, and German preferred. 

**French Tier 2 is trading tight to NPS,** versus where the sector average is for this relationship. Whilst this is hardly new news (considering the continued support from domestic French buy-and-hold Tier 2 investors), Tier 2 valuations have become even richer relative to NPS more recently (see Exhibit 3), with Tier 2 tightening outpacing that of NPS. Whilst we'd expect French Tier 2 to remain 'rich' versus peer NPS/T2 relationships, we would expect it to edge back closer to historical levels. With Tier 2 tight to NPS, which in turn trades wider than average to preferred, we see the most outright value in NPS. Naturally we recommend buying those issuers with lower net issuance needs, which are SocGen and BPCE with just €2bn and €1.8bn for this year. 

**Barbelling Tier 2 and preferred senior in Spain:** Santander NPS spreads are relatively tight, at only 52% of Tier 2 spreads compared to the sector average at 66%. The tight level is supported in our view by Santander not needing to issue further NPS for MREL, creating scarcity value. Given its NPS trades flat to BPCE while its Tier 2 trades 50bp wide, we see value in Santander Tier 2. BBVA's preferred senior also trades tight on limited supply, at just 42% of NPS spreads and flat to Santander preferred, while its NPS trades wider. Between the two, Santander's preferred offers the best relative value, in our view. 

**German NPS trades relatively rich still** , trading closer to preferred than the sector average and further away from Tier 2. Whilst Germans' NPS benefits from lower loss-given failure due to the greater volume of outstanding NPS, we expect this relationship to change for Commerzbank in particular as it refinances maturities over time into preferred senior. Commerzbank NPS trades flat to SocGen and generally in-line with French banks while its preferred senior is not only wider but has a much steeper curve (see Exhibit 5). 

**We recommend buying** BPCE € 1.375% 26 and SocGen € 1.125% 25 NPS; Santander € 2.5% 25 Tier 2; Santander € 1.375% 22, Commerzbank € 1% 26 and € 1.5% 28 preferred. 

**We recommend selling** BPCE € 2.875% 26 and SocGen € 2.625% 25 Tier 2; Santander € 1.125% 25 NPS and BBVA € 0.625% 22 preferred.
