- Since Monday, February 4, positioning has shifted. Within G10, the largest short is in CAD; the largest long is in USD. 

- **USD long positioning increases:** Global macro funds reduced their USD long positions in a slight reversal compared to the previous two weeks. Traders' sentiment improved on the USD in contrast to other currencies aside from the JPY and CHF, which were broadly unchanged. US-based ETFs also increased their USD holdings. Increasingly long USD positioning increases the risks for a sharp USD downside move if US data deteriorate. 

- **CAD positioning moves to solidly short** : Traders' sentiment turned further negative on the CAD. We estimate that CFTC non-commercial accounts reduced CAD positioning. The rate of change of our CFTC estimates is influenced by data release delays due to the U.S. government shutdown. These developments were moderately offset by improved sentiment. CAD, NZD, and AUD sentiment and CFTC positioning were all reduced this week in line with our long-term view on these nations' housing sectors' vulnerability to globally rising cost of capital. 

- **AUD positioning more short:** We estimate that US-based ETF flows reduced their AUD holdings and CFTC AUD positioning became more long. Traders' sentiment also turned further negative on the AUD. We like short AUD positions against BRL (where the new administration has emphasized commitment to market-friendly measures) and JPY (where we see the potential for gains in a risk-off environment). _Due to the US government shutdown, we estimate latest CFTC positioning using spot FX moves._

## Methodology 

## **Methodology** 

**IMM** – We use the U.S. CFTC’s IMM report to track positioning of non-commercial traders. 

**Toshin** – The Toshin accounts are Japanese foreign currency investment trusts that seek yield abroad by investing in foreign assets on a currency-unhedged basis. **TFX** – The Tokyo Financial Exchange (TFX) measures Japanese currency trading on margin accounts, and comprises an estimated 10% of the retail margin market. **Beta** – As an alternative proxy for positioning, our Beta-Tracker measures onemonth rolling betas of global macro hedge funds’ daily returns on major currency indices. 

**ETF** – Our MS FloMo provides daily indications on cross-border equity flows from US ETFs 

**Sentiment** – The Daily Sentiment Index is a survey of traders and measures shortterm market sentiment among futures traders. It gathers opinions on all active US futures, Eurozone interest rates, and Eurozone equities futures market.
