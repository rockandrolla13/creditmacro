## **The Week Ahead** 

**In the euro area,** we expect €23bn issuance (from Germany, France, Spain and Italy) against just €0.6bn coupons and €16bn redemptions. Average gross supply for the same week of the past three years has been €16.4bn. 

**In the UK,** UKT 1.75% 2057 will be issued for £1.5bn, against no coupons and redemptions. 

**In the US,** new 30y TIPS will be issued for $8bn, which will settle in the week after next along with the settlement of the new 2y, 5y, 7y. $8.5bn coupons and $94bn redemptions will be paid in the week after next. 

**In Japan,** 20y JGB will be issued for ¥1000bn, against ¥4bn coupons and no redemptions. 

**In Australia,** ACGB 2.75% Jun-21 and ACGB 2.50% May-30 will be issued for $400mn and $900mn, respectively, against no cash flow. 

**In New Zealand,** NZGB 2.75% Apr-37 will be issued for $200mn, against no cash flow. 

**In Canada,** 30y CAGB linker will be issued for an estimated $1bn, against no coupons and redemptions. 

## **The Coming Five Weeks** 

**In the eurozone,** we expect about €86bn of supply in the coming five weeks against €14.7bn of coupons and €72.7bn redemptions. This results in a net cash surplus of €1.3bn.
