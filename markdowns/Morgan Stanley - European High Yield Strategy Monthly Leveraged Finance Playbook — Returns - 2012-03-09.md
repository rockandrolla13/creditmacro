## **Returns** 

European high yield returned 1.8% over the past month, led by beta, with CCCs returning 4.2%. The industrials sector outperformed other non-financials with a 2.5% total return. IG corporates, by contrast, returned 1.7%. 

## **Defaults** 

At the end of January, the trailing 12-month European speculative-grade default rate declined to 2.7%, according to Moody’s data.
