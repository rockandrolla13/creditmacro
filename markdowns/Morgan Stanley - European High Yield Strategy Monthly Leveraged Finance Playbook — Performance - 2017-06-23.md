## **Performance** 

EUR HY total returns continued to increase over the last month and now stand at 3.4% YTD. Across the ratings spectrum, single-Bs outperformed, with the B-BB basis compressing by another 35bp. At a sector level, healthcare and industrials performed well while energy and consumers posted the weakest total returns over the month.

## **Supply and Demand** 

European HY issuance across currencies currently stands at €5.2bln in June, down ~15% compared to May. EURdenominated HY supply was more subdued at just €3.3bln, taking YTD volumes to €30bln (+40%Y). On the demand side, European HY mutual funds saw modest net inflows, providing further technical support to the asset class.

## **Defaults and Ratings** 

Trailing 12m issuer-weighted default rates in European HY remained stable at 2.5% as of the end of May. Meanwhile, ratings drift (upgrades versus downgrades) fell slightly to 6.5%. Moody’s trailing 12-month global recovery rates continued to rise, with senior unsecured bond recoveries reaching 50%, the highest level since late 2010.
