## **European Economics Weekly | Europe** 

## Ongoing Weakness 

In the euro area, we expect continued declines in the data, and will focus on the ECB's take on the economy in the minutes. In the UK, we expect robust data and more Brexit talks. 

**Euro Area | Weak surveys & ECB minutes:** All the most-watched business surveys in the euro area will be released next week. We think the **PMIs** will rise slightly, mostly supported by services, but we project that both the **German Ifo and ZEW** will decline. **Final inflation data** will also be in focus, with detail on the recent methodological changes still to be released. We had an upside surprise in core last month, but with below-trend growth in the euro area lasting for longer, we now don't expect meaningful acceleration in coming months. This makes the ECB more dovish. We now think the first rate hike will only take place in 2020. We'll look at the **ECB minutes** for more detail around the perception of risks following the recent change in language. 

**UK | Robust data but no rebound:** After below-trend growth, below-target inflation and dovish MPC commentary this week, we expect more robust data next week. However, this reflects past strength rather than a rebound from recent weakness. We expect a largely unchanged **labour market** print with a steady below-trend unemployment rate and cycle high pay growth, but with a slower economy starting to show up in a sharp deceleration in employment growth. In the key January **public finance** data, we expect a strong print, reflecting robust 2017-18 self assessment tax receipts. On Brexit, we expect **ongoing talks** between the UK, EU and Ireland, ahead of the next key **Parliamentary debate** , currently scheduled for February 27.
